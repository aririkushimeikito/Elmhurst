Our team

Doctors from the neighborhood, for the neighborhood.

Meet the providers who will know your name, your family and your history — and who speak the languages you speak at home.

Dr. Choudhury Hasan
Internal Medicine
English · Spanish · Bengali · Hindi
View full profile →
Hover for full bio →

Dr. Hasan earned his medical degree from Ross University School of Medicine and completed his internal medicine residency at Flushing Hospital Medical Center in Queens. Board-certified with more than a decade of experience, he also practices as a hospitalist — caring for acutely ill patients admitted to the hospital. That gives him an unusually complete view of medicine: he has seen how conditions unfold at their most serious, and he brings that depth and vigilance back to your everyday primary care.

Patients describe him as thorough, honest, and a breath of fresh air. He takes the time to hear your whole story, explains the reasoning behind every recommendation, and builds a real plan you can actually follow — then checks back to make sure it's working. For Dr. Hasan, medicine is personal: he cares for every patient the way he'd want his own family cared for, in the language they're most comfortable speaking.

Dr. Ferdous Salauddin
Internal Medicine
Bengali · Hindi · English
View full profile →
Hover for full bio →

Dr. Salauddin completed his internal medicine residency at Interfaith Medical Center in Brooklyn (One Brooklyn Health) and is board-certified by the American Board of Internal Medicine. Like Dr. Hasan, he practices as a hospitalist — caring for seriously ill patients admitted at Mount Sinai Hospital of Queens — and also serves as a clinical instructor at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, helping train the next generation of physicians. That high-acuity, academic experience sharpens his judgment in the exam room, so small problems are caught early and nothing important gets missed.

With additional postgraduate training from the Royal Colleges of Physicians in the UK (MRCP) and a special interest in endocrinology, he is especially strong with diabetes, thyroid, and other hormone-related conditions. He's known for his steady, reassuring presence and his patience with questions big or small — he takes the time to understand each patient's home life, culture, and goals before recommending a plan, so the families he serves always receive thoughtful, up-to-date care.

Keerthana Rajagopal, PA
Physician Assistant
Tamil · Hindi · English
View full profile →
Hover for full bio →

Keerthana earned her physician assistant degree at Barry University in Florida and brings warmth and genuine curiosity to every visit. She has a gift for making patients feel heard — turning a routine check-up or a worrying symptom into an unhurried, judgment-free conversation.

Attentive and deeply patient-centered, she takes pride in the small things: remembering your last visit, explaining results in plain language, and making sure you leave knowing exactly what comes next. Passionate about preventive care and health education, she is especially committed to helping first-generation and multilingual families navigate the system with confidence.
