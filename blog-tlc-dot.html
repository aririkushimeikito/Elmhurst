TLC & DOT physicals: what to bring and how long it takes

If you drive for a living, your medical exam shouldn't slow you down. Here's how to get cleared quickly — often the same day.

If you drive for a living in New York, your medical exam shouldn't slow you down. Here's exactly what to bring and what to expect so you can get cleared and get back on the road — often the same day.

Which exam do you need?

  • TLC medical exam — required to drive a taxi or for-hire vehicle (Uber, Lyft, livery) in New York City.
  • DOT / CDL physical — required for commercial drivers, completed by a certified examiner and recorded on the federal medical certificate ("med card").

What to bring

  • A valid photo ID and your driver's license (and current TLC or CDL documents if you have them).
  • Glasses or contact lenses and hearing aids if you use them — bring them to the exam.
  • A list of your medications, and letters from your specialists if you manage a condition like diabetes, high blood pressure, sleep apnea or a heart condition.
  • Any prior forms the agency gave you.

What we check

The exam is straightforward and usually takes about 20 to 40 minutes. It typically includes:

  • Vision and hearing screening.
  • Blood pressure and pulse.
  • A urine test (for the DOT exam, this screens for certain medical conditions — it is not a drug test).
  • A general physical exam and a review of your health history.

Tips to avoid delays

  • Manage your blood pressure. A very high reading can hold up your certificate. If you take BP medication, take it as usual and bring the details.
  • Bring documentation for any ongoing condition — a recent note from your specialist can prevent a repeat visit.
  • Bring your corrective lenses. You're allowed to wear them for the vision test.

In most cases we complete your forms during the visit so you leave with what you need. If something needs follow-up, we'll tell you exactly what to do.
