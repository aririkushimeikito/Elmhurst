Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you take my insurance?+
We accept most major insurance plans, plus Medicare and Medicaid — and we offer transparent cash-pay pricing if you're uninsured. Not sure? Call or text us and we'll check for you.
What languages do you speak?+
Spanish, Bengali, Hindi, Urdu and English — from the front desk to the exam room to your follow-up texts.
Can I get a same-day appointment?+
Yes — we hold same-day slots, plus evening and weekend hours, so you can be seen when you actually need it.
Do you do immigration and TLC physicals?+
We do — civil surgeon immigration exams (I-693), TLC/CDL/DOT clearances, and school, sports and work physicals, often same-day.
Are you taking new patients?+
Always. New patients age 16 and up are welcome — book online, by phone, or on WhatsApp.
Do you have evening or late hours?+
Yes — we're open late, until 7:30 PM Monday through Friday, plus Saturday afternoons. Our evening and late hours make it easy to see a doctor after work or school.
Do you have a Hindi-speaking doctor?+
Yes. Our doctors and staff speak Hindi, Bengali, Spanish, Urdu and English, so you can talk about your health in the language you're most comfortable in.
Can you help with weight loss?+
We offer medically supervised weight-loss and nutrition support as part of your primary care — including guidance for diabetes, blood pressure and healthy, sustainable weight management.
Do you offer mental health care or psychiatry?+
Yes — we provide mental health care and psychiatry for anxiety, depression, stress and sleep, right alongside your regular medical care, in your language.
