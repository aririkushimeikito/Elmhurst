What happens at your visit

Simple, unhurried, and in your language.

No confusing forms or rushing. Here's what a visit with us actually looks like, start to finish.

First, we hear your whole story

Your visit starts with an unhurried conversation about your health, history, medications and what matters to you — in the language you're most comfortable in.

Then, your exam & screenings

We check your vitals, do a focused physical exam, and order the right labs and screenings for you — blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol and age-appropriate cancer screenings.

Finally, your plan — together

We explain every result in plain language and build a clear, realistic plan with you — plus any referrals, coordinated on your behalf so you never chase paperwork.
