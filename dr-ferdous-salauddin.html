Dr. Salauddin sees adults at Elmhurst Medical on 74th Street. A lot of what he does is metabolic medicine — diabetes, prediabetes, thyroid, PCOS, cholesterol, weight — which in practice means he spends real time explaining lab results to people who were handed a printout and told “everything's fine” or “your sugar's a little high,” with no third sentence.

He completed his internal medicine residency at Interfaith Medical Center in Brooklyn (One Brooklyn Health) and is board-certified by the American Board of Internal Medicine. Like Dr. Hasan, he practices as a hospitalist — caring for seriously ill patients admitted at Mount Sinai Hospital of Queens — and serves as a clinical instructor at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai. With additional postgraduate training from the Royal Colleges of Physicians in the UK (MRCP) and a special interest in endocrinology, he is especially strong with diabetes, thyroid, and other hormone-related conditions.

He is known for his steady, reassuring presence and his patience with questions big or small. He takes the time to understand each patient's home life, culture, and goals before recommending a plan — and he explains every step in the language you are most comfortable in.
