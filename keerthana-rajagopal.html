Keerthana is a physician assistant at Elmhurst Medical on 74th Street in Elmhurst, Queens. She is often the person you see when something comes up today: a sore throat that will not quit, a cut that might need stitches, a rash, a fever, a UTI, or a work or school note. She also sees primary care patients for physicals, preventive screening, hair and skin concerns, and mental health check-ins.

She earned her physician assistant degree at Barry University in Florida and brings warmth and genuine curiosity to every visit. She has a gift for making patients feel heard — turning a routine check-up or a worrying symptom into an unhurried, judgment-free conversation. Attentive and deeply patient-centered, she takes pride in the small things: remembering your last visit, explaining results in plain language, and making sure you leave knowing exactly what comes next.

Passionate about preventive care and health education, she is especially committed to helping first-generation and multilingual families navigate the health system with confidence.
