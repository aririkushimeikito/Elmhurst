Dr. Hasan is an internal medicine doctor at Elmhurst Medical, on 74th Street between Roosevelt and Broadway. Internal medicine means adult primary care — he is the doctor you see for the things that build up slowly and quietly (blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol, thyroid, weight) and the doctor you call when something new shows up and you are not sure how worried to be.

He earned his medical degree from Ross University School of Medicine and completed his internal medicine residency at Flushing Hospital Medical Center in Queens. Board-certified with more than a decade of experience, he also practices as a hospitalist, caring for acutely ill patients admitted to the hospital — which gives him an unusually complete view of medicine, from the first warning sign to the serious complication he works to help you avoid.

The way he practices is straightforward: you get the whole appointment. He explains what your numbers mean in plain language, tells you what actually changes them, and is honest about what the evidence does and does not support. Patients describe him as thorough, honest, and a breath of fresh air. He also writes and medically reviews much of the health library on this site.
